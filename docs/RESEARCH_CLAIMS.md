# Research claims

## Claims supported by the current workflow

- PV generation and electrified heating demand can be compared in consistent hourly energy units under stated assumptions.
- Seasonal and intra-annual temporal mismatch can be quantified with explicit metrics.
- The sensitivity of mismatch to HLC, COP, PV area and heating base temperature can be tested reproducibly.
- A synthetic benchmark can be compared with a real-weather case without changing the model logic.
- The workflow can identify when detailed storage/flexibility or building simulation is likely to be necessary.

## Claims intentionally not made

- prediction of actual energy use in a specific building;
- field validation of heating demand or PV yield;
- performance prediction of a specific heat-pump product;
- HVAC or district-heating simulation;
- optimal system sizing;
- grid reinforcement or distribution-network impacts;
- occupant comfort prediction.

## Recommended description in applications

> Self-initiated, publicly documented Python research demonstrator for screening climate-driven temporal mismatch between PV generation and temperature-driven electrified space-heating demand, with unit-consistent energy accounting, sensitivity analysis, automated tests and an observed-weather input pathway.
